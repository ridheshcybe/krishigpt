  Limitations:
    - Fusion tabular inputs are simulated from the XGBoost confusion matrix (Dirichlet noise) because no paired sensor/leaf dataset exists; fusion accuracy therefore measures rule arbitration, not field data.
    - The XGBoost 'healthy' class excludes 61,263 binary=0 rows that fall outside the strict moisture/rainfall window; they are bucketed into stress_generic, which caps healthy precision.
    - The paddy archive contains augmented flips/rotations of the same leaves, so the CNN score is a within-dataset estimate.

    - MobileNetV2 emits logits (no in-head softmax): CrossEntropyLoss consumes logits, so softmax is applied at inference (predict_proba, fusion_inference, validation) instead.
